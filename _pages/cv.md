  * Advisors: Dylan Connor (co-chair, Stanford/ASU); Wei Li (co-chair, ASU); Mark Ellis (UW)

Research Interests
======
**Urban spatial demography and hyper-local population health.** Building longitudinal, high-resolution health and population data by linking health records, socioeconomic, built-environment, and demographic microdata, and developing scalable spatial-epidemiologic and GeoAI frameworks to quantify how economic dynamics, built environments, and community resources shape health inequities over the life course.

**Decision-ready GeoHealth for science and policy.** Bridging GIScience and population health to produce reproducible, policy-facing GeoHealth analytics — partnering with public agencies, healthcare systems, and NGOs to monitor health trends, identify high-risk neighborhoods, and evaluate place-based interventions using privacy-aware, longitudinal geospatial workflows.

Research Experience
======
* **Postdoctoral Associate** — University of Colorado Boulder, CU Population Center & Institute of Behavioral Science (Nov. 2024–Present)
  * PI, CU Population Center Seed Grant: *Neighborhood Mortality Regimes Across the Life Course: Community Wealth, Family Stability, and Youth Mortality* — directed a spatial-demography pilot study integrating geocoded death certificates with novel tract-level community-wealth databases, and developed multilevel spatial models and Bayesian causal inference in R/Python.
  * NIH/NIA R01, *Addressing the Midlife Mortality Crisis: Place-Based Modeling, Trend Analysis and Policy Interventions* — built large-scale geospatial health datasets from over 10 million confidential mortality records across 16 states, and developed machine-learning longitudinal models of mid-life mortality using Bayesian multinomial, multilevel, and spatial autocorrelation models.
* **Data & Policy Analyst** — Acumen LLC / SPHERE Institute (Nov. 2022–May 2024)
  * Cleaned, processed, and analyzed large health datasets, including over 20 million Medicaid and nursing-facility records, for the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the Center for Program Integrity, and the Nursing Home Quality Initiative.
  * Developed a sequence-analysis method with clinicians and policy experts to detect fraud claims and drug abuse.
* **Graduate Research Associate** — Arizona State University, Spatial Analysis Research Center (Aug. 2018–Nov. 2024)
  * NIH R21, *Trends, transitions, and well-being in small-town America* — established typologies of longitudinal demographic and socioeconomic change using sequence analysis, machine-learning clustering, and geospatial analysis.
  * NSF, *The creeping disaster along the coast* — analyzed built-environment features and urban landscape metrics for 709 U.S. commuting zones using remote sensing and the Ztrax dataset.
  * Led the research design and analysis team for the *COVID-19 and a World of Ad Hoc Geographies* book project, including a community-level, race-specific social vulnerability index and California statewide hot-spot mapping of anti-Asian incidents with the Stop AAPI Hate coalition.
