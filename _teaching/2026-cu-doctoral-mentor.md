Mentored a Geography PhD researcher through the ideation and development of a successful competitive funding proposal, resulting in the award of an IBS Summer Research Grant. Designed and delivered one-on-one methodological training in advanced machine learning algorithms (sequence analysis) to build the student's independent quantitative research capacity, and established a structured doctoral supervision framework — including project timelines, weekly progress meetings, and milestone tracking — to ensure rigorous research execution. Guided the translation of research findings into academic and public-facing outputs, leading the student toward a targeted academic presentation and the development of research exhibition materials.
