Research

In U.S. mid-life mortality, most of the variation sits inside counties, not between them, and county averages hide it. The same is true of much of the variation in wealth and opportunity. My work brings that hidden variation into view, then asks what local conditions explain it and where action would help.

Neighborhoods and population health

I link geocoded death records from state vital-statistics offices, now more than 10 million records across 16 states, and harmonize them to census tracts over time. That lets us follow mid-life mortality neighborhood by neighborhood since 2005 and see which places stay resilient and which fall behind. I am extending the same infrastructure to youth mortality and to cognitive health in later life.

Wealth, opportunity, and place

Wealth is far more unevenly spread across places than income, and it shapes what a community can offer the people who grow up and grow old there. With collaborators, I build long-run measures of community wealth and study how they relate to mobility, health, and aging.

Migration, skills, and regional economies

The knowledge economy concentrates rewards in a few places and for some workers more than others. I study the STEM workforce by nativity and gender: where foreign-born workers were educated, where they settle, and why women in STEM, particularly immigrant women, earn less even in the strongest labor markets.

Data and methods for small-area demography

Much of this depends on tools that don't exist yet, so I build them: population-weighted crosswalks across census geographies, a 100 m population grid, imputed locations for national survey microdata, and pipelines that combine sequence analysis, machine learning, Bayesian spatial models, and remote sensing. The public versions are on the data page.

Research in practice

Before my postdoc I was a data and policy analyst at Acumen / SPHERE Institute, analyzing more than 20 million Medicaid and nursing-facility records for the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. During COVID-19 I worked with the Stop AAPI Hate coalition to map anti-Asian incidents across California and to build a social vulnerability index specific to Asian American communities.

Mentoring

At CU Boulder I am faculty advisor of record for a geography PhD student's funded summer research project, and I supervise two PhD and one undergraduate research assistants. At ASU I mentored two undergraduate honors theses.
