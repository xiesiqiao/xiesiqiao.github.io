Data

Free to use with citation. Each dataset is archived at CU Scholar, the University of Colorado's repository, with a DOI and a readme.

Crosswalks between commuting zones and historical geographies, 1990–2020

Population-weighted crosswalks that link 2020 commuting zones and PUMAs to 1990, 2000, and 2010 commuting zones and 2010 PUMAs, so labor-market data can be compared across decades. Built from the 100 m population grid below.

CSV tables of overlaps, shares, and totals

Xie, S. (2025). Crosswalks between Commuting Zones and Historical Geographies (1990–2020) [Data set]. University of Colorado Boulder.

Get the datadoi:10.25810/5XP7-YE36

100 m population grid, 2020 Census

U.S. 2020 Census counts allocated to 100 × 100 m cells, for analysis below the county and tract level.

State GeoTIFFs, EPSG:5070 (NAD83 / Conus Albers)

Xie, S. (2025). 100×100m Population Grid (2020 Census) [Data set]. University of Colorado Boulder.

Get the datadoi:10.25810/PB2D-P578

GEO-NSCG, 2021 five-year estimates

The foreign-born sample of the National Survey of College Graduates with probability-based metro-area locations, for studying where highly skilled immigrants live and work.

Microdata and documentation

Xie, S. (2025). GEO-NSCG 2021 5-Year Estimates [Data set]. University of Colorado Boulder.

Get the datadoi:10.25810/5D5P-FX20
