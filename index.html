Siqiao Xie 谢斯峤

Spatial demographer and computational social scientist
Postdoctoral Associate, Institute of Behavioral Science, University of Colorado Boulder

My research asks how the places people live shape health and opportunity across the life course, from mid-life mortality and cognitive aging to wealth and migration. I link administrative records, surveys, and satellite imagery into longitudinal small-area data to identify the local conditions behind population health disparities, and I make much of that infrastructure public.
